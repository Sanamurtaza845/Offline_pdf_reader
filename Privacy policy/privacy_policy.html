Privacy Policy

App Name: Offline PDF Reader

Developer: Innovexa Technologies

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Offline PDF Reader. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use the app.

2. Information Collection

Offline PDF Reader does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to our servers. The app does not require users to create an account or log in.

3. PDF Files and Local Storage

PDF files selected by users are stored locally within the application's private storage on their device. These files are not uploaded to our servers.

Users can manage their documents within the application, including viewing, renaming, and deleting files.

4. File Sharing

The application allows users to share PDF files using the device's available sharing options. When a user chooses to share a file, the selected third-party application may process that file according to its own privacy policy.

5. App Preferences

Certain application preferences, such as the selected theme, are stored locally on the user's device. This information is not transmitted to our servers.

6. Internet and Third-Party Services

Offline PDF Reader does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services. The app does not use third-party services to collect personal information.

7. Data Security

Your PDF documents remain in the application's local storage. We recommend keeping your device secure and maintaining backups of important documents.

8. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
